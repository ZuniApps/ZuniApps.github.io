Made for my own setup

  • In Z Palace, a few things only work with my own equipment, and the public version leaves them out: films kept on an external drive, Kodi on my projector, bringing notes and keys in from my Obsidian vault, and sending playlists to YouTube.
  • Whole apps that stay mine: my phone's home screen (Z Launcher), a sync for my e-reader's highlights, and two small helpers for my own phone.
