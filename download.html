Download

Get Z Palace.

Free while it is in early testing. Neither version is in an app store yet, so each shows a warning the first time you open it. The steps past it are short, and they are below.

Mac

Version 1.18 · zip, about 90 MB
Download for Mac
  1. Open the zip and drag Z Palace into Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS says it cannot check the app. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, press Open Anyway next to Z Palace, then Open. On an older Mac: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open.
  3. Or in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Z Palace.app"
  4. Press Start a new library and pick a folder.

Android

Version 0.18 · apk, about 19 MB
Download for Android
  1. Open the downloaded file. Android asks you to allow installs from this source: press Settings, switch on Allow from this source for your browser or Files app, go back, and press Install.
  2. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, press Install anyway.
  3. Open Z Palace and choose Account.

Updates

Z Palace tells you when a new version is out: a line at the top of Settings on the Mac, and in Account on Android. Come back here and download it again. Your library is not touched.

Signing in

Optional. Once it is installed, sign in under Settings › Account with Google or your email, to share a note or a board with someone you choose. Your library itself stays on your devices.
