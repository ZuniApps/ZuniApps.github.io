Download Z Palace

Free while it is in early testing. Pick your device, install, then open Settings › Account and sign in with Google or your email.

Mac

Download for Mac

  1. Open the downloaded zip and drag Z Palace into your Applications folder.
  2. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app (it isn't from the App Store yet). Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and press Open Anyway next to Z Palace, then Open. On an older Mac: right-click the app › Open › Open.
  3. Prefer Terminal? xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Z Palace.app" does the same thing.
  4. Press Start a new library, pick a folder, and you are in.

Android

Download for Android

  1. Open the downloaded file. Android asks to allow installing from this source: press Settings, switch on Allow from this source (for your browser or Files app), go back and press Install.
  2. If a warning says the app is not from Play Protect's list, press Install anyway. The app is not in the Play Store yet.
  3. Open Z Palace, choose Account, and sign in.

Updates

Z Palace tells you when a new version is out (a line at the top of Settings on the Mac, in Account on Android). Come back to this page and download it again; your library is not touched.
