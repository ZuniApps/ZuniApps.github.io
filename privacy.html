Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

What stays on your devices

Your notes, cards, boards and everything in your library are stored on your own devices and in the storage you choose for syncing. We do not receive or read them.

What the account service keeps

If you create an account, the account service (Supabase, hosted in the EU) keeps your email address, the name and colour you choose for your profile, who you are friends with or have blocked, and which shared items you belong to and with which role. Items you share are stored encrypted.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture (the openid, email and profile permissions). We use them only to create and identify your account.

What we never do

We do not sell or share your data, show ads, or track you across other sites.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings › Account in the app. This removes your profile and your memberships from the account service.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/ZuniApps.
